Privacy Policy

Created & Called · last updated 21 August 2026

Created & Called ("we", "us") sells faith-inspired minimalist apparel and prints. This policy explains what personal data we collect and how we use it. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the shop.

What we collect

Email address — only if you choose to join our mailing list. Order & delivery details (name, address, contact) — when you place an order, handled through the marketplace you buy from (e.g. Etsy) and our print partner to fulfil and ship your order. We do not collect payment card details ourselves; payments are processed by the marketplace.

How we use it

To fulfil and deliver your orders, to respond to your messages, and — if you opted in — to send occasional updates about new designs. We never sell your data.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers needed to operate: the marketplace (Etsy), our print-on-demand partner (Monster Digital) for fulfilment, and our email provider (Mailchimp) if you subscribed. Each processes your data only to provide their service.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time via the link in any email. To ask what data we hold, or to have it corrected or deleted, email us and we will action it.

Cookies & analytics

Our website is a simple static site and does not set tracking cookies. Marketplaces and platforms we use (Etsy, Pinterest, Mailchimp) apply their own cookie and privacy policies on their sites.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email info@createdcalled.uk.
